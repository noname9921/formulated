# Prerequisites

This platform presupposes a foundational level of mathematical literacy on the part of the User. Prior to engaging with any material presented herein, Users are strongly advised to possess a confident and working knowledge of the following competencies:

- **Fundamental Arithmetic** — Proficiency in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division across integers, negative numbers, and mixed operations
- **Fractions, Decimals, Ratios, and Percentages** — The ability to convert between, compare, and operate on these numerical forms with fluency
- **Elementary Algebra** — Comfort with variable manipulation, solving linear equations and inequalities, and understanding algebraic expressions
- **Introductory Coordinate Geometry** — Familiarity with the Cartesian plane, point plotting, distance between points, and the concept of slope
- **Basic Mathematical Reasoning** — The capacity to follow logical steps, interpret mathematical notation, and construct simple proofs or justifications

While the Platform endeavours to build knowledge from foundational principles, the absence of the competencies outlined above may substantially impede a User's ability to engage with and benefit from the material provided. Users who identify gaps in the above areas are encouraged to seek supplementary resources before proceeding.
